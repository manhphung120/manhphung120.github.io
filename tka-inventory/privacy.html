Privacy policy — TKA inventory

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Who uses this app

TKA inventory is used by one person only, its owner, with the owner's own Google accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

  • Gmail (scope gmail.metadata): message headers (From, To, Subject, Date) and labels. Not the message body and not attachments.
  • Google Drive (scope drive.metadata.readonly): file and folder names, types, owners, sizes and dates. Not file contents.

Access is read-only. The app never sends, deletes, modifies or shares anything in Gmail or Drive.

Where data is stored

Sign-in tokens and the metadata inventory are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server operated for this app, and they are not sold, shared or transferred to anyone else. The owner may review the inventory with an AI assistant on the owner's own initiative.

Google API Services User Data Policy

TKA inventory's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to revoke access

Remove "TKA inventory" at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The owner can also delete the local tokens and inventory at any time.

Tiếng Việt (tóm tắt)

Công cụ chỉ do chủ sở hữu dùng với tài khoản Google của chính mình; chỉ đọc metadata Gmail (người gửi, người nhận, chủ đề, ngày, nhãn) và metadata Drive (tên, loại, chủ sở hữu, ngày), không đọc nội dung thư, tệp đính kèm hay nội dung file; không gửi, xoá, sửa hay chia sẻ gì. Token và danh mục chỉ lưu trên máy của chủ sở hữu, không bán, không chia sẻ cho ai. Thu hồi quyền tại myaccount.google.com/permissions.
